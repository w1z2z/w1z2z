# Vitaly Kochergin

Backend engineer focused on **Node.js, TypeScript, NestJS and PostgreSQL**. Building commercial software since 2021.

I design and develop backend systems, data models and integrations for e-commerce, fintech and SaaS products. My experience includes asynchronous processing, idempotent file ingestion and complex business workflows. I have also coordinated a team of five developers.

I use AI-assisted development to accelerate implementation, with a growing focus on system design, architecture, security and product development — from business requirements to deployment and operation.

## Selected projects

Focused backend demos exploring transactions, service communication and background processing.

- **[Balance service](https://github.com/w1z2z/balance-service)** — transactional balance updates, row-level locking and operation history.
- **[User and audit services](https://github.com/w1z2z/microservices-grpc)** — NestJS services communicating over gRPC, with separate PostgreSQL databases and best-effort audit delivery.
- **[Marketplace offers API](https://github.com/w1z2z/marketplace-offers-api)** — offer retrieval using PostgreSQL and Redis, with BullMQ background processing.
